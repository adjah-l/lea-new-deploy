Lawrence E. Adjah, MBA, M.Ed, M.Div., is a community leader, social entrepreneur, and ordained minister whose work sits at the intersection of relational health, social infrastructure, faith, and community innovation. He is the Founder and CEO of the Family Dinner Foundation, where he built one of the world's largest belonging and relational wellness initiatives, connecting more than 50,000 young adults across 40 cities through scalable models of shared meals, small-group formation, and mutual care.

A Presidential Leadership Scholar (2024), former McKinsey consultant, and All-American track and field athlete, Lawrence holds degrees from Harvard, Stanford Graduate School of Business, UT Austin, and Gordon-Conwell Theological Seminary. His perspective is shaped by a rare blend of insight: he sees people like a pastor, valuing their humanity before their utility; he sees systems and problems like a strategy consultant, structured, analytical, and solutions-oriented; and he sees opportunities like an entrepreneur and investor, courageous, adaptive, and driven by possibility.

Lawrence brings together theological depth, business strategy, and cross-sector fluency to address loneliness and social fragmentation at scale. His 5C Mutual Care Framework © provides a replicable blueprint for relational ecosystems across cities, health systems, universities, corporations, churches, and neighborhoods. He is also a best-selling author, seasoned marriage counselor, Bible narrator (NIV), and global speaker and host whose work through the Just Heal, Bro tour and other platforms has served thousands worldwide.

A trusted leader of leaders across business, ministry, and the nonprofit sector, Lawrence is known for uniting systems-level strategy with theological and relational insight, combining the business acumen of a CEO with the interpersonal wisdom of a pastor. His work strengthens individuals just as effectively as it strengthens communities and institutions for the common good.

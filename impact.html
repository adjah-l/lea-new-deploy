How to read these figures

The figures describe cumulative program reach reported by Family Dinner Foundation across gatherings, city communities and related experiences. They are not a count of current members, monthly active users or independently audited unique individuals.

Public sources do not always use the same city total. The George W. Bush Presidential Center profile describes nearly 50,000 people across more than 30 cities and three continents; Family Dinner Foundation’s current public reporting uses approximately 50,000 people across roughly 40 cities and three continents. The difference reflects reporting date and scope, not a claim that every participant can be deduplicated from a single current database.

Preferred public wording: “Family Dinner Foundation reports cumulative reach of approximately 50,000 people across roughly 40 cities and three continents.”

What is supported

Claim boundaries

  • Founder role: Lawrence’s public title is intentionally presented on this site as Founder and CEO of Family Dinner Foundation.
  • Presidential Leadership Scholar: Lawrence was named to the 2024 class by the Presidential Leadership Scholars program.
  • Stanford pilot: the Bush Center profile identifies Stanford among the universities that piloted the community-building work.
  • 5C framework: Family Dinner Foundation publishes the canonical description of Commitment, Communication, Connection, Crisis and Celebration.
  • Audiobook: Lawrence narrated the complete New International Version Bible audiobook, distributed through major audiobook platforms.

What is not claimed

Honest limits

  • The reach figures do not establish causation, clinical outcomes or population-level reduction in loneliness.
  • A program pilot does not establish that every adaptation has the same effect.
  • The 5C framework is a practice model; its results depend on facilitator quality, duration, context and participation.
  • Participation, attendance and engagement are not treated as interchangeable with belonging or mutual care.
  • Product demos and illustrative data are not presented as verified adoption metrics.

Editorial policy

How this page is maintained

Material numbers should be dated, defined and linked to a source. When sources disagree, this page states the range or explains the difference. When a claim cannot be substantiated, the preferred action is to narrow or remove it rather than repeat it more loudly.

Before you start

General instruction
This guide gives you frameworks and practical tips to support your wellness goals. It is not built from a one-on-one consultation or a formal personal training engagement, so tailor it to your current fitness level and health status.
Your doctor
As with all exercise programs, the strong recommendation is that you consult with your doctor before starting any new training or fitness program.
Healthy capacity
Adjust the weight for every exercise to your healthy capacity: the weight where you can do the exercise well without compromising your health or safety, in the short term or the long term.

The standard

Lift at least three days a week.

Where are you working out?

Three days: Monday, Wednesday, Friday. Go up in weight after each set. Example: 15 reps at 45 lbs, 12 reps at 60 lbs, 10 reps at 80 lbs. Where you see “or failure,” take the last set to failure.

Consumption

The standard

80/20: five days clean, two days your choice. Whole foods, no fried foods. One gallon of spring water a day.

Water

A gallon a day

  • For energy, nutrient transport, and the cleansing process.
  • Start of the day, fill a gallon container full. Drink at least half by noon. As you progress, aim for the whole gallon by noon.
  • Aim to be done by 6:30 p.m. so you can sleep without having to get up.
  • If anything is left at the end of the day, pour it out and start again.
  • Invest in two items to make it more likely you follow through: a gallon container and a water filter.

Rhythm

80/20

  • Monday through Friday late afternoon (5:00 p.m.), eat clean. Friday night through Sunday night, no special restrictions.
  • Balance is built in, and you earn the weekend. It is psychologically sustainable.
  • This has served me since I was 18. I believe in lifestyle plans, not diets, which are not sustainable.

On your plate

Protein and greens

  • High protein, healthy fats, whole, unprocessed foods.
  • Eggs and seafood (least inflammatory) during the week, paired with salads and greens. High-quality, organic, grass-fed beef.
  • Sweet potato when you take in carbohydrates.
  • Avocado and olive oil only.

Leave out during the week

What to avoid

  • No bread. No fried foods. No dairy outside of eggs. No sugars.
  • No processed foods. If it is not whole, if it comes in a box, likely not.
  • Protect your health: no soy, no seed oils, no sucralose, no high-fructose sugars.

Beyond the minimum

Eating window
Intermittent fasting, 14 to 18 hours, to minimize your consumption window and limit insulin spikes and midday crashes. If your last meal is at 8:00 p.m. (the latest I recommend), your earliest next meal is 10:00 a.m. Start with 14 hours for three weeks, then test 16. If it is too steep, stay at 14 for another three weeks, then move up to 16 (8:00 p.m. to noon).
Carbohydrates
Low, with a daily deadline: 50 to 100 grams a day and none after 5:00 p.m. Pair your heavy lift days with your higher-carb (100 gram) days.
Timing
Nothing but water after 8:00 p.m. Give yourself at least two and a half to three hours between your last consumption and sleep. Descend your food intake through the day. After a workout, high protein immediately.

Check in honestly.

Every day in iToro: walked, lifted, water, clean eating. Nobody is grading you. You are building a record you can trust.

Before you start

Frameworks, not formulas
My goal is to provide you with frameworks and practical tips to support your spiritual wellness goals for this season of your life. This guide is not based on a formal one-on-one spiritual guidance or discipleship relationship.
With your brothers
Pursue these goals with a community with accountability, so you not only strengthen your relationship with God, you strengthen your relationship with people at the same time. That is what your five are for.
Your doctor
For your safety, consult with your doctor before you fast or make any significant shift in what you consume. If something medical applies to you, adjust the fast. Obedience is not the same as recklessness.

1

Pray

The standard

Every day. Live with the brothers once a week. One prayer request a week, minimum. Pray for every brother within 24 hours.

Set it up

A Bible
A physical one is recommended. NIV or ESV translation.
A time and a place
Set a specific daily time and a specific place for your prayer and reading. Schedule it in your calendar.
Your brothers
Commit to your group. Check in every day, and encourage the brothers who have shared theirs.

Monday prayer points

Every Monday, pray through these while you fast.

  1. Yourself
  2. Your spouse
  3. Your children
  4. Your extended family
  5. Your friends, by name
  6. Your friends, by name, who do not yet know Jesus

Then as the Spirit leads.

Kinds of prayer

Praise and thanksgiving

Gratitude

Words of gratitude and thankfulness to God for His innumerable blessings in our lives.

Confession and repentance

Forgiveness and change

Acknowledging our sin before God and affirming His truth, then committing, tangibly, to turn from it.

Lament

Sadness and grief

A prayer for God’s help to deliver from distress, suffering, and pain.

Supplication

Your desire

A request to God that asks Him to fulfill a need of your own.

Petition and intercession

Another’s desire

A request to God that asks Him to fulfill a need on behalf of someone else.

2

Fast

The standard

Every Monday and the first Friday of the month, sunup to 3 p.m. or 6 p.m. You decide. Last Monday: 24 hours, water only.

In fasting, we are intentionally subordinating our hunger for that greater hunger. We are clearing our plates, our hearts, our minds to have more space to connect more deeply with God.

But fasting is not only about silencing the noise of our life; fasting is about connecting with the power of God. It is about strengthening the power of our Spirit and weakening the power of our flesh over our lives.

When Jesus speaks to His disciples about fasting, He begins by saying, “When you fast,” not “if” you fast (Matthew 6:16).

However, this kind does not go out except by prayer and fasting.

Matthew 17:21, NKJV

The Monday fast

The night before
Decide your hours, 3 p.m. or 6 p.m., and decide what you will break your fast with.
First thing
No food from sunup. Drink a cup of lemon water, take a serving of electrolytes, then a cup of water with a dash of salt.
By noon
Drink at least half of your gallon. Add electrolytes and salt as needed.
While you fast
Pray through the Monday prayer points. When the hunger comes, let it send you back to prayer.
In secret
Do not look somber. Wash your face. It is not for others to see, only your Father, who sees what is done in secret (Matthew 6:16-18).
Breaking it
Gently. Bone broth, soup, a smoothie, a light fruit like berries, or a vegetable. If you want something more substantial, eggs or fish.

Have on hand: a stainless steel or glass canister for water, electrolytes, iodized sea salt, pink Himalayan salt, and lemon.

The last Monday: 24 hours

The hours
From 6 p.m. or 8 p.m. the evening before (you decide) through the following day.
What you take in
Water only. Keep your electrolytes and salt.
Ending it
Be extremely careful in how you break this fast, given your body has not processed food in some time. Doing it wrong can lead to severe pain and illness. Start with broth, soup, or berries before anything heavier.
Medical
If anything medical applies, consult your doctor first.

If you need to adjust

A partial fast
A complete fast is not the only fast. A partial fast eliminates certain foods and drinks for a specific amount of time.
The Daniel Fast
Vegetables and water, drawn from Daniel 1:12 and Daniel 10:3. A common modification is no meat, whole foods only, and only water.
The heart of it
There is no one way to do a fast. The point is the heart and the objective of your fast.

3

The Word

The standard

One meditative Scripture, committed to heart by week seven. One Bible chapter a day. If you do not know where to start, take Proverbs.

Your quiet time

1. Read
Read the chapter slowly and silently, twice. Read it out loud, slowly. Underline the verses that hit you. Write out your questions about anything you do not know or understand.
2. Meditate
Sit with your eyes closed, in silence, for two minutes, reflecting on what you read.
3. Journal
Not required. Example: write three things you are grateful for.
4. Pray
Pray as a response to what you read.
5. Check in
Mark your day in iToro and encourage the brothers who have shared theirs.

This is not a formal framework for interpreting the Bible, by design. It is about establishing a foundation of familiarity and consistency.

Every time you fast, you are making a deposit.

My prayer is that with each day, you would experience God more deeply. May God lead you, and may fruit come from this place. In Jesus’ Name. Amen and Amen.

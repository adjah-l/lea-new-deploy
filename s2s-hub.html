How the seven weeks run

  • Profile → Challenges → Strength to Strength. Your challenge home. It shows the day you are on, what is up next, and today's check-in.
  • Every week, by Monday. Share one prayer request with your group. Pray for every brother's first request within 24 hours, and later ones within 48. Only a real prayer counts: a voice prayer or a live prayer session.
  • Every day. Check in: walked, lifted, water, clean eating, fasted, your chapter, your Scripture. Sharing details with the group is your choice; notes are always private.
  • Once a week, live. We gather in a prayer room. A highlight and a struggle. Then we pray.
  • The clock. The 49 days start when Lawrence launches the cohort. Reminders come as app notifications, so turn them on.

The expectations

Three areas of strength.

Spiritual strength

  • Pray. Every day. Live with the brothers once a week. One prayer request a week, minimum. Pray for every brother within 24 hours.
  • Fast. Every Monday and the first Friday of the month, sunup to 3 p.m. or 6 p.m. Last Monday: 24 hours, water only. GuideFasting & prayer points
  • Monday prayer points. Yourself. Your spouse. Your children. Your extended family. Your friends by name. Your friends by name who do not yet know Jesus. Then as the Spirit leads.
  • Study. One meditative Scripture, committed to heart by week seven. One Bible chapter a day. If you do not know where to start, take Proverbs.

Physical strength

  • Walk. At least fifteen minutes in the morning, five days a week. Minimum three days; if three, do thirty minutes.
  • Work out. Lift at least three days a week. GuideAt-home workout
  • Consumption. 80/20: five days clean, two days your choice. Whole foods, no fried foods. One gallon of spring water a day. GuideConsumption

Relational strength

  • Commitment. Five men. Seven weeks. You show up, every week.
  • Connection. We gather to pray together in real time, at least once a week.
  • Communication. We all hear from one another at least once a week through prayer.
  • Crisis. If a brother shares a crisis, every brother calls him within 24 hours. No man suffers alone.
  • Celebration. When God moves, we mark it. We call within 24 hours for that too.

Questions

Before you ask

When does my seven weeks start?
When Lawrence launches the cohort in iToro. You will get an email and an app notification. Until then, start the inputs on your own: pray, walk, read a chapter. Nothing is wasted.
Which gathering time will we use?
Your group decides. The standard options are Mondays at 7:30 a.m., 8:30 a.m., or 9:00 p.m. Eastern. You told us your preference in the intake; the confirmed time comes with your group assignment.
I have a medical condition. Should I fast?
Consult your medical professional first and adjust. Obedience is not the same as recklessness. The fasting guide will cover common adjustments.
Do I have to use makairos?
No. It is a recommendation for your one daily Scripture. Any method that gets the Word into your heart works.
What if I miss a day?
Check in honestly and keep going. Nobody is grading you. You are building a record you can trust, and your brothers are praying for you either way.
Can I join later, or bring a brother?
Groups form as intakes come in. Send him to lawrenceadjah.com/s2s. Once a cohort is launched, new men go into the next one.

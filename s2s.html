Expected outcomes

Stronger. Measurably.

Spiritually

Stronger spiritually

A more honest and consistent prayer life. A transformative fasting life. Measurable victory over the issues of the flesh.

In the Word

Stronger in the Word

At least one new promise of God from Scripture hidden in your heart.

Relationally

Stronger relationally

At least one new man who knows you and invests in your life. Not a contact. A brother.

Physically

Stronger physically

Healthier by every tangible measure. Your body is a temple of the Holy Spirit, and we treat it that way.

Expected inputs

Three areas of strength.
A few clear inputs in each.

Where you see Guide, a guide is sent to you after intake.

Spiritual strength

  • Pray. Every day. Live with the brothers once a week. One prayer request a week, minimum. Pray for every brother within 24 hours.
  • Fast. Every Monday and the first Friday of the month, sunup to 3 p.m. or 6 p.m. Last Monday: 24 hours. GuideFasting & prayer points
  • Study. One meditative Scripture, committed to heart by week seven. One Bible chapter a day.

Physical strength

  • Walk. At least fifteen minutes in the morning, five days a week.
  • Work out. Lift at least three days a week. GuideAt-home workout
  • Consumption. 80/20. Whole foods, no fried foods. One gallon of spring water a day. GuideConsumption

Relational strength

  • Commitment. Five men. Seven weeks. You show up.
  • Connection. We gather to pray together in real time, every week.
  • Communication. A highlight and a struggle, every week.
  • Crisis. If a brother shares a crisis, you call within 24 hours.
  • Celebration. When God moves, we mark it. We call within 24 hours for that too.

All together, once a week

We all gather together for live prayer in iToro once a week. Your group of five keeps each other accountable in between. The Monday times below are preferences, not guaranteed options. Choose what works best in the intake; we will do our best to accommodate it and confirm the time once groups are set.

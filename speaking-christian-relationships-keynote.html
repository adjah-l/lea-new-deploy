The framework

Three relationships. One integrated life.

Christian formation is relational. We receive life from God, learn to love our neighbors, and steward the person God has entrusted to us. Lawrence teaches these three relationships as distinct but inseparable: faith that never touches our relationships is incomplete, and relational wisdom detached from God loses its center.

The message can be taught as a sermon, keynote, workshop or multi-session experience. Every format is adapted to the congregation, institution and theological context.

Pastoral boundary: these talks offer biblical teaching and practical formation. They do not replace licensed mental-health care, medical care or individualized pastoral counseling.

Topics

Messages for real life

With God

Prayer, Scripture, obedience, discernment, worship, spiritual warfare and learning to remain with God through every season.

With other people

Friendship, marriage preparation, family, forgiveness, conflict, mutual care and the habits that make Christian community trustworthy.

With yourself

Identity, emotional honesty, discipline, stewardship of the body and refusing to confuse achievement with worth.

Faith and work

Integrating belief and vocation without asking work to carry the weight of identity, belonging or salvation.

Go From Scared to Prepared

Surfacing the unspoken.

Most couples spend more time planning their wedding than they do their marriage. While the ceremony lasts a day, the union is intended for a lifetime. The 100 Marriage was created to bridge that gap.

Developed through years of pastoral counseling and spiritual direction with over 100 couples, this curriculum provides a practical, faith-rooted framework for surfacing and aligning unspoken expectations.

Through 100 biblically grounded decisions and declarations, you and your partner will navigate the core pillars of a flourishing union:

  • Surface and align your unspoken expectations
  • Make 100 biblically grounded decisions + declarations before marriage
  • Build a shared foundation of understanding, trust, and agreement
  • Prepare not just for a wedding, but for a marriage

Whether you are single and preparing, engaged and dreaming, or married and strengthening your bond, The 100 Marriage is your toolkit for building a life as you build a love.
